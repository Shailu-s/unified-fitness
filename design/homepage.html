Kindle paper

Warm off-white #FAF7F1, never pure white — paper reflects, screens emit. Ink is warm near-black #1F1B16, not #000, which is harsh on light ground.

Search in the thumb zone

Pinned to the bottom above the home indicator. On a 6.1" screen the top edge needs a hand shuffle; the bottom is where the thumb already rests. Logging is the app's primary verb, so it belongs where the hand is.

Empty ring centre

The rings now carry progress by shape alone; the three numbers live in the boxes below. Apple's own colours throughout — #FA114F, #92E82A, #1AD7E5.

Steps as an open line

No box, no border, no fill — it sits on bare paper above the ring, reading as a stated fact rather than a widget. Being the only unboxed element on the screen is what gives it weight.

Legend removed

The three boxes name and colour-code every metric, so the dot legend was saying it twice. One accessory off.

One caution on the colours

Apple tuned these for a black screen, where they glow. On paper white the green in particular loses contrast. If it reads washed out to you, say so — I can darken them slightly while keeping them recognisably Apple.

Vertical budget — the gap, fixed

Content is 524px on an 852px screen, leaving 328px free. That slack was pooling into one dead gap above the search bar. It now distributes through four elastic spacers (68 / 96 / 96 / 68), and the ring grew 250 → 290px to use the room. On a taller 6.7" phone the gaps stretch; on a 5.4" they compress. Nothing is positioned by guesswork.

Almost no fitness app is light-first

The category defaults to black. Paper white is genuinely differentiating and easier to read in daylight, which is when your user checks their steps.
